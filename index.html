How I work

Five ways I
create value.

Good learning design keeps people in view while helping organizations solve problems, support performance, and move important work forward.

01

Analyze

I look beyond the initial request to understand learners, tasks, constraints, and the performance an experience needs to support.

02

Design

I translate those insights into clear objectives, organized content, meaningful practice, and intuitive structures.

03

Develop

I build thoughtful, accessible learning experiences and practical resources that are polished, purposeful, and ready to use.

04

Implement

I refine details, collaborate with stakeholders, and prepare solutions to work effectively in their intended context.

05

Evaluate

I use feedback, review, and reflection to identify what can be strengthened, then revise the experience with intention.

A little about me

Thoughtful work starts with attention.

I’m Sarah, an instructional designer finishing my master’s in Instructional Psychology and Technology. I’m drawn to the space where learner needs, organizational goals, and good creative work meet.

I bring empathy, structure, and a practical curiosity to each project. My goal is to create experiences that respect people’s time, make expectations visible, and help them move forward with confidence.
