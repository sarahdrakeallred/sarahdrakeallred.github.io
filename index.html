How I work

Five ways I
create value.

Good learning design keeps people in view while helping organizations solve problems, support performance, and move important work forward. I use an iterative process, drawing on feedback and reflection to refine solutions and make them more useful.

01

Analyze

I look beyond the initial request to understand learners, tasks, constraints, and the performance an experience needs to support.

02

Design

I translate those insights into clear objectives, organized content, meaningful practice, and intuitive structures.

03

Develop

I build thoughtful, accessible learning experiences and practical resources that are polished, purposeful, and ready to use.

04

Implement

I refine details, collaborate with stakeholders, and prepare solutions to work effectively in their intended context.

05

Evaluate

I use feedback, review, and reflection to identify what can be strengthened, then revise the experience with intention.

A little about me

People are at the heart of my work.

Hi, I’m Sarah, an instructional designer with a master’s degree in Instructional Psychology & Technology from Brigham Young University and a bachelor’s degree in Human Development. My interest in people, especially how they learn, grow, and navigate challenges, shapes the way I approach instructional design.

I grew up in Arkansas and now call Utah home. Outside of design, I’m a wife and mom of three. I love hiking Utah’s mountains and watching my kids try new things, take on challenges, and discover what they’re capable of.
